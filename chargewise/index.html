What this browser demo models

The browser solves a continuous minimum-cost flow problem in 15-minute slots, with fixed plug assignments, per-vehicle and shared power limits, 90% efficiency, departure energy targets and charger outages. A separate check recomputes energy and physical limits from the schedule. Peak minimisation uses a feasibility search, followed by energy-cost minimisation at that peak.

For the afternoon outage, the base schedule before noon is frozen. Those allocations cannot be changed by the replan. Greedy comparison charges each available van immediately, subject to the same connection and outage.

This lean version uses constant charging-power limits. It does not model state-of-charge taper, forecast electricity prices or call an LLM. The full application uses OR-Tools and includes taper, uploads, forecasting and Nemotron tool calls. All data here are synthetic; no charger is controlled. The bill includes energy and the larger of the prior and scenario peak, multiplied by the illustrative demand tariff. It is not an annual savings estimate.
