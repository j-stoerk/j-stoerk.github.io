Compute the effective through-plane thermal conductivity of a wet graphite anode coating at porosity 0.30 with particle diameter 17 µm, and the same for a wet NMC cathode at porosity 0.30 with 8 µm particles. Also show the Knudsen-reduced dry-air conductivity in a separator with 0.40 porosity and 0.3 µm pore diameter. Finally, verify that graphite flake reorientation at S=1 gives the c-axis floor (6 W/mK) and at S=0 gives the isotropic average (102 W/mK), while NMC remains isotropic (2.5 W/mK) regardless of S.
